A modern, offline-first Point of Sale and inventory management system designed for reliability, speed, and ease of use in retail environments.

**Product Vision**
This system enables small to medium retail businesses to process transactions and manage inventory seamlessly, even without internet connectivity.

**Target Users**
- Cashiers: Fast transaction processing
- Store Managers: Inventory oversight and reporting
- Business Owners: Multi-location management and analytics

**Core Capabilities**
- Process sales transactions offline
- Real-time inventory tracking
- Multi-tenant support for multiple locations
- Automatic sync when online
